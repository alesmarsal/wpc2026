# Data pack: Why do OPEC, the IEA and the EIA disagree about oil demand in 2026?

KAPSARC Knowledge Sprint, 25th WPC Energy Congress, Riyadh, 12 October 2026
*Generative AI for Evidence-Based Oil Markets* (Aleš Maršál and Emre Hatipoglu, KAPSARC)

## Get everything in two steps

1. **Download this repository** as a ZIP: green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.
2. **Download the two IEF comparisons** from the IEF website (the IEF does not allow reposting its files):
   - September 2026: https://www.ief.org/_resources/files/news/comparative-analysis-of-monthly-reports-on-the-oil-market/september-2026/ief-comparative-analysis-09-2026.pdf
   - February 2026: https://www.ief.org/_resources/files/news/comparative-analysis-of-monthly-reports-on-the-oil-market/february-2026/ief-comparative-analysis-02-2026.pdf

Save them next to the other files as `2026-09_IEF_comparison.pdf` and `2026-02_IEF_comparison.pdf`.

## Editions

All numbers are frozen at the **September 2026** editions (IEF comparison of 14 September 2026), plus the IEF comparison of the **February 2026** editions, written before the Strait of Hormuz disruption began on 28 February 2026. Exception: the IEA balance and observed stocks come from the IEA's report of 17 June 2026. Newer editions (e.g. the EIA STEO of 6 October) are deliberately not used.
