### Observed stocks, "Implied balance" (PDF p. 52)

> "Global observed stocks showed a smaller draw in April than estimated in last month's Report, declining by 2.5 mb/d rather than -3.9 mb/d, based on more complete, though still preliminary, data from OECD member countries. […] Taking into account a 1.3 mb/d reduction in oil on water, estimated global observed stocks in May fell by 4.6 mb/d at the time of writing."

| Observed stock changes (mb/d) | Apr-26 | May-26 |
|---|---|---|
| OECD industry stocks | −0.6 | −0.4 |
| OECD government stocks | −2.7 | −2.5 |
| Non-OECD crude stocks* | −0.4 | −0.3 |
| Selected non-OECD product stocks** | −0.4 | −0.2 |
| Oil on water | 1.6 | −1.3 |
| **Total observed stock changes** | **−2.5** | **−4.6** |

"*Observed non-OECD crude stocks are from Kayrros, adjusted NGLs from JODI, plus estimated data for South Africa's Saldanha Bay from Kpler. **JODI data adjusted for monthly gaps in reporting, latest data for March 2026, plus Fujairah and Singapore inventories." Negative = stocks fell.
